## Values or costs far too high or negative – is the unit right?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=12; readme=covered; cluster=prices-costs-17,setup-ui-19,periods-19; features=unit-detection-conversion,source-unit,source-start-values,supported-units,price-definitions-table -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Einheit und Startwerte prüfen, Jahresordner fehlt wegen Einheit. -->

Usually a unit doesn't fit: on the source, on the price or on a start value. Check them in the source's detail view and then correct the values.
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; code=lib/calculation.js; forum=562155; forum=1097521; forum=600948; forum=823571 -->

| Wrong | Result | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| unit `kWh`, device delivers `Wh` | everything 1,000 times too large | the unit the device delivers |
| meter reading labelled `kW` | values grow far too fast | meter reading in `kWh`, power in `W` |
| price in cents, `28.5` instead of `0.285` | costs 100 times too high | price in euros per unit |
| start value in `Wh`, price in `kWh` | consumption strongly negative | start value in kWh |
<!-- quelle: code=lib/calculation.js; code=main.js; code=admin/i18n/en.json; forum=1239306; forum=486811; forum=549375; forum=796798 -->

At the top of the detail view is the type, for example "Meter reading · kWh", and below it the "Total". Compare it with your meter, then check "Unit" and "Price definition" under "Settings". The "Problems" tab reports unsuitable start values as "Start value does not fit".
<!-- quelle: code=src-admin/src/SourceDetail.tsx; code=src-admin/src/i18n/en.json; code=lib/source-overview.js -->

Stored values don't change with the unit. Afterwards, fix the total with "Align meter reading" and recalculate the costs with "Recalculate costs from date", see [Correcting wrong values](../02.anleitungen/02.zaehlerstaende-korrigieren.md#correct-wrong-too-high-negative).
<!-- quelle: code=lib/source-actions.js; issue=#640; issue=#947 -->

## After an update or restore, values are 0 or SourceAnalytix is missing

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=7; readme=missing; cluster=upgrade-migration-other-02,upgrade-migration-other-03; features=source-hidden-reset-keys,reset-manual-correction,migration-legacy-cumulative-states -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Nach Restore Pakete neu installieren; Roh-Schlüssel prüfen. -->

After a restore, the adapter itself is often missing, because a backup saves data, not program code. Reinstall SourceAnalytix from "latest"; the restored instance then keeps running. An update does not change your values.
<!-- quelle: forum=1352377; forum=1329319; forum=1172189; interview=21 -->

After a restore, the total and the settings may come from different points in time:

1. Open the source and compare the "Total" with your meter.
2. If it doesn't match, use "Align meter reading".
3. On the "Problems" tab, catch up a "Period rollover missed" with "Recover".
<!-- quelle: code=src-admin/src/SourceDetail.tsx; code=lib/source-overview.js; code=src-admin/src/i18n/en.json; issue=#640; issue=#1269; forum=587649; forum=914227 -->

**See also:** [Where is the data?](../02.anleitungen/05.betrieb-und-wartung.md#data-back-up-move-new)

<a id="costs-stay-0-not-calculated"></a>

## Costs stay 0 or are not calculated

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=6; readme=partial; cluster=prices-costs-16; features=source-calc-costs,price-invalid-unit-price,source-price-definition,output-cost-vs-earnings,source-unit -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Preis, Einheit, Costs-Haken. -->

Costs are only created with the "Costs" tick on the source and a valid "Price per unit". With the cost type "Earnings", they are under `earnings`, not `costs`.
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; forum=847112; forum=931989; issue=#750 -->

1. **"Costs" on?** Check it in the detail view under "Settings".
2. **Price definition chosen?** Otherwise the "Problems" tab reports it.
3. **Is the price a number?** Otherwise SourceAnalytix calculates with 0 and logs `Price definition <name> has no valid unit price …`.
4. **Price from a datapoint?** If it delivers nothing valid, the "Price per unit" you entered applies; if that is empty, the costs stay 0.
<!-- quelle: code=src-admin/src/SourceDetail.tsx; code=main.js; code=admin/i18n/en.json -->

Afterwards, "Recalculate costs from date" recalculates the costs with the price history.
<!-- quelle: code=src-admin/src/i18n/en.json; issue=#947 -->

**See also:** [Assigning prices](../02.anleitungen/03.preise-und-kosten.md#assign-prices-create-custom-tariffs) · [PV yield and feed-in](../02.anleitungen/01.quellen-und-einheiten.md#track-pv-yield-feed-earnings)

<a id="huge-consumption-after-power-outage"></a>

## Huge consumption on power sources after a power outage

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=4; readme=missing; cluster=cumulative-power-07; features=source-power-integration,calc-calc-block -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Lücken und letzte Leistung; Abhilfe. -->

With power, the last value applies until a new one arrives. If the device fails without reporting 0, the last power is extrapolated over the whole pause. "Maximum gap between power values (minutes)" prevents this.
<!-- quelle: code=lib/calculation.js; code=main.js; issue=#1266; forum=511607; forum=404253 -->

1. Open the power source.
2. Under "Settings", enter a value for "Maximum gap between power values (minutes)" well above the longest normal interval between two values.
3. Click "⚡ Apply". Longer gaps then count as 0.
<!-- quelle: code=src-admin/src/SourceDetail.tsx; code=src-admin/src/i18n/en.json; code=main.js; issue=#1266; issue=#1285 -->

> [!WARNING]
> Leave the field empty if your device only sends on change. Otherwise the consumption of long steady stretches is lost.
<!-- quelle: code=admin/i18n/en.json; interview=9 -->

If no value arrives for three hours, the "Problems" tab reports "Power source has not reported for a long time". Repair values already booked as described under [Correcting wrong values](../02.anleitungen/02.zaehlerstaende-korrigieren.md#correct-wrong-too-high-negative).
<!-- quelle: code=lib/source-overview.js; code=src-admin/src/i18n/en.json -->

<a id="so-many-decimals"></a>

## Why so many decimal places?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=3; readme=partial; cluster=prices-costs-13; features=calc-rounding,rounding-instance-defaults,source-decimals -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Rundung und Standardwerte. -->

The source's rounding is probably set to `-1`, meaning unrounded. The default is 3 places for consumption and 2 for costs. Change them in the source's detail view under "For experts".
<!-- quelle: code=admin/jsonConfig.json; code=lib/calculation.js; code=main.js; code=src-admin/src/SourceDetail.tsx -->

The values under "Main settings" › "Rounding" only apply to new sources. How many places, zeros or thousands separators you see is also up to your display, such as VIS.
<!-- quelle: code=admin/i18n/en.json; code=lib/calculation.js; forum=786213 -->

## Known bugs in older versions – please update

Update to the current version. This documentation only describes that one; which bug was fixed in which version is in the [changelog](https://github.com/DrozmotiX/ioBroker.sourceanalytix/blob/main/README.md#changelog). Many forum reports, such as duplicated values after a reset or missing day rollovers, concern old versions.
