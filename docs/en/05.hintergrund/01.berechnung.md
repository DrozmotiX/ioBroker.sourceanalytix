## Why do the values differ from my meter, portal or evcc?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=10; readme=missing; cluster=cumulative-power-05,prices-costs-18; features=source-power-integration,source-average-power,calc-consumption-formula,source-cumulative-meter -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Unterschiede durch Messzeitpunkt, Integration, Einheit und Startwerte. -->

Usually for one of four reasons: the source is a power value that rarely sends, the portal assigns consumption to a different time, the device measures differently from your meter, or start values and unit don't fit.
<!-- quelle: forum=731502; forum=1348168; forum=1168164; forum=488316 -->

| Cause | How to recognise it | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| power (W) that only sends every few minutes | large deviation | use a total counter in kWh or make it send more often |
| a pause of a power source | consumption is missing or too high after an outage | "Maximum gap between power values (minutes)" |
| assigned to a different time | days differ, the sum over several days matches | nothing, both are right |
| the device measures differently, e.g. a Shelly 3EM per phase | doesn't match the meter in the basement | read the meter itself, for example with an optical reading head |
| start values, unit or total wrong | negative, too high or offset | [Correcting wrong values](../02.anleitungen/02.zaehlerstaende-korrigieren.md#correct-wrong-too-high-negative) |
<!-- quelle: forum=572093; forum=488316; forum=731502; code=lib/calculation.js; issue=#1266; forum=1348168; forum=682759; forum=1168164; forum=528812 -->

SourceAnalytix books consumption on the day it was measured. A wallbox or evcc often books a whole charging session on the day it ends. Over several days this evens out.
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; forum=1348168; forum=682759 -->

**See also:** [Meter or power?](../01.erste-schritte/02.erster-zaehler.md#datapoint-enable-meter-kwh-power)

## How does SourceAnalytix convert watts to kWh?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=7; readme=missing; cluster=cumulative-power-04,prices-costs-27,cumulative-power-27; features=source-power-integration,source-average-power,output-timestamps-ack,source-cumulative-meter -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Riemann-Summe/Mittelwert, Update-getriebene Berechnung, Pausen. -->

With every new value: the previous power times the time since the previous value. With "Average power values", SourceAnalytix uses the average of the previous and the new value. The energy is added to the total.
<!-- quelle: code=lib/calculation.js; code=main.js; issue=#1166 -->

| Time | Value | without average | with average |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | 0 W | starting point | starting point |
| 10:30 | 1,000 W | 0 Wh | 250 Wh |
| 11:00 | 1,000 W | 500 Wh | 500 Wh |
| Total | | 0.5 kWh | 0.75 kWh |
<!-- quelle: code=lib/calculation.js -->

Without the average it suits devices that switch abruptly and only send on change; with the average, sensors that send regularly. It calculates on every written value, even if it stays the same; the last value applies until the next one.
<!-- quelle: code=lib/calculation.js; code=main.js; issue=#1166; interview=9 -->

Pauses:

- The first value after activation or a restart is only the starting point.
- If no value arrives for a long time, the last one is extrapolated, unless "Maximum gap between power values (minutes)" is set.
- At midnight, the energy up to 0:00 is booked to the old day.
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; issue=#1254; issue=#1266; issue=#1301 -->

Each stretch is priced at the price that applied at the time of the value.
<!-- quelle: issue=#1300; code=main.js -->

## How is consumption calculated, and why is currentYear lower than my meter reading?

<!-- abschnitt: prio=B; gewicht=1; readme=covered; cluster=meter-readings-05; features=calc-consumption-formula,output-cumulative-reading,source-start-values -->
<!-- muss beantworten: Formel Verbrauch = Zählerstand minus Startwert. -->

Because `currentYear.consumed.05_currentYear` is this year's consumption, not the meter reading. It is the total minus the "Year" start value. The meter reading is in `cumulativeReading`.
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; issue=#641 -->

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Total | 12,480 kWh |
| "Year" start value (reading on 1 January) | 11,900 kWh |
| `05_currentYear` | 580 kWh |
<!-- quelle: code=main.js -->

SourceAnalytix sets the start values itself at every period rollover. If you activated during the year, the year counts from that day, unless you enter the reading from 1 January as the [start value](../01.erste-schritte/02.erster-zaehler.md#start-values-unit).
<!-- quelle: code=main.js; code=lib/calculation.js -->
